# Price Suggestion Logic — Client Review

## Current Understanding

We understand that when a customer brings in a used device, our buyback offer should be calculated step by step, like this:

**Used market value → Device condition → Storage/variant → Battery → Age → Damage/repairs → Accessories → Demand/market → Buyback margin → Suggested offer**

In plain terms:

1. **Used market value** — what the device is roughly worth in the current resale market.
2. **Device condition** — overall wear and tear (like new, good, fair, broken, etc.).
3. **Storage/variant** — higher storage or premium variants are worth more.
4. **Battery** — better battery health adds value; weak batteries reduce it.
5. **Age** — how old the device is.
6. **Damage/repairs** — screen or body damage, or replaced parts, lower the value.
7. **Accessories** — original box, bill, and accessories add a small premium.
8. **Demand/market** — popular, in-demand models hold value better.
9. **Buyback margin** — the amount we keep between the resale value and what we pay the customer.
10. **Suggested offer** — the final price we quote the customer.

- **How should battery health affect the price?** Should the value drop sharply below a certain health level (e.g., under 80%), or reduce gradually?
- **How should screen/body damage affect the price?** Should the deduction be based on the estimated repair cost, or a flat percentage of value?

- **Should local demand or current market prices affect the price?** Should we adjust based on what similar devices are selling for locally or online?
- **Are there any specific pricing rules your team currently uses that we should include?** Any rules of thumb your team applies when valuing a device by hand.

Take a device with a **used market value of ₹50,000**:

| Step | Adjustment | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Used market value | Starting point | ₹50,000 |
| Condition | Good overall condition, visible wear | − ₹5,000 |
| Storage/variant | Higher storage than the base model | + ₹3,000 |
| Battery | Battery health around 75% | − ₹2,000 |

| Damage/repairs | Minor body scratches, no screen damage | − ₹1,000 |
| Accessories | Original box and bill available | + ₹1,000 |
| Demand/market | Popular, in-demand model | + ₹2,000 |
| Buyback margin | Amount we keep | − ₹10,000 |

This shows how a device's market value could be adjusted step by step. The exact amounts and percentages for each step are what we need your team to confirm.
